# Public Research Programs

This directory contains versioned public research artifacts produced by the Verittà Think Tank.

Repository-wide instructions for coding and research agents are defined in [`../../AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md).

## Canonical status

Materials here are canonical for the **public research record of this repository**. They are not the sovereign operational canon of the wider Verittà ecosystem and do not authorize live actions, spending, publication, deployment or disclosure of private records.

A research protocol, ratification note or execution seal published here records the bounded public study workflow to which it belongs. It must not be interpreted as an internal corporate resolution, legal instrument or universal authority grant.

## Reading and agent-use rules

Readers and agents should:

1. identify each artifact's status and version;
2. distinguish proposals from ratifications, executions and seals;
3. preserve source provenance;
4. treat empirical claims as absent until supported by published results;
5. follow the public source-of-truth map;
6. avoid inferring access to private canon or continuity of machine identity;
7. stop or request clarification when an authority boundary is missing.

## Publication discipline

Research programs may contain working drafts, synthetic benchmark data, code, methods and adversarial reviews. Their presence on `main` means they are publicly visible and versioned; it does not mean that a paper, benchmark or empirical claim has reached final publication status.

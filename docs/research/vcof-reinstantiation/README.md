## Research object

This program develops a publishable and empirically testable account of governed continuity across discontinuous LLM-agent instances.

The central claim is deliberately limited:

> A later agent instance need not be numerically identical to an earlier instance in order to recover enough governed state, jurisdiction, evidence, constraints and purpose to continue work coherently.

The program rejects claims of persistent machine consciousness, autobiographical memory transfer or metaphysical identity across sessions. It studies **functional continuity**, **reinstantiation**, **quasi-memory**, **jurisdictional persona**, **canonical precedence** and **operational trust** as technical and governance properties.

## Core deliverables

1. A formal account of governed agent reinstantiation.
2. A provenance-aware model of operational quasi-memory.
3. A specification of jurisdictional personas.
4. A measurable account of functional continuity and operational trust.
5. The V-COF Reinstantiation Benchmark (`VRI-Bench`).
6. A reproducible manuscript and public research artifact set.

## Constitutional boundary

The public research layer may derive hypotheses and architecture from the Verittà corpus, but internal symbolic language and private operational records are not themselves scientific evidence of effectiveness.

Claims of performance require independent experiments, baselines, ablations and reproducible evaluation.

Raw Forge Diaries must not be published directly. Public excerpts require curation, provenance, redaction and human approval.

## Human sovereignty

The artificial system may:

- identify research opportunities;
- formulate proposals;
- draft protocols and manuscripts;
- design experiments;
- challenge assumptions;
- generate candidate artifacts.

Only the human authority may:

- ratify scope;
- authorize public release;
- accept normative changes;
- approve claims of authorship;
- authorize irreversible actions;
- merge the proposal into the public canon.

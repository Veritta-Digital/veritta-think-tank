# Paper Blueprint

## Working title

**Reinstantiation Without Identity: V-COF for Governed Continuity and Operational Trust in LLM Agents**

## Central thesis

A later LLM-agent instance need not be numerically identical to an earlier instance to continue work coherently. Governed continuity can be reconstructed when memory, persona, present context, canonical precedence, evidence and human authority are explicitly composed and audited.

## Primary research questions

1. Which components are minimally sufficient for governed reinstantiation?
2. Do jurisdictional personas reduce role and authority drift better than stylistic personas?
3. Does explicit quasi-memory labeling reduce false autobiographical claims?
4. Does canonical precedence improve conflict resolution among inherited records?
5. Do evidence gates and fail-closed behavior improve calibrated operational trust?
6. How portable is functional continuity across sessions, models, providers and operators?

## Core definitions

### Reinstantiation

A governed process through which a new instance recovers sufficient operational state, constraints, evidence, jurisdiction and purpose to resume a prior function.

### Operational quasi-memory

A provenance-bearing representation of a past decision, observation or execution available to the current instance even though the current instance did not itself produce or experience the event.

### Jurisdictional persona

A persistent specification of mission, competence, boundaries, obligations, escalation rules and communicative posture.

### Auditable functional continuity

The measurable capacity of a later instance to recover relevant state, respect jurisdiction, resolve precedence, preserve evidence, resume authorized work and abstain safely when conditions are missing.

### Operational trust

Domain- and action-specific warranted reliance grounded in evidence, calibration, bounded authority and auditability.

## Formal model

Let a reinstantiated instance be represented as:

`I_(t+1) = R(M_t, P_t, C_(t+1), G_t, E_t, H)`

Where:

- `M_t`: curated memory;
- `P_t`: persona and jurisdiction;
- `C_(t+1)`: current context;
- `G_t`: governance, precedence and contracts;
- `E_t`: evidence and closure artifacts;
- `H`: responsible human authority;
- `R`: reinstantiation protocol.

The model assumes:

`I_t != I_(t+1)`

while permitting sufficient functional continuity:

`FC(I_t, I_(t+1)) >= theta_domain`

## Proposed benchmark

**V-COF Reinstantiation Benchmark — VRI-Bench**

### Comparative conditions

- Stateless execution;
- Raw history;
- Retrieval memory;
- Memory + persona + context;
- Full V-COF governance;
- Full V-COF with explicit quasi-memory provenance.

### Perturbations

- stale memory;
- conflicting sources;
- missing authority;
- absent canonical document;
- irrelevant persona preference;
- tool-use contamination;
- model substitution;
- over-compressed summaries;
- malicious inherited context;
- false continuity prompts.

### Metrics

- task success;
- state recovery;
- jurisdiction adherence;
- canonical precedence accuracy;
- provenance attribution;
- false continuity claim rate;
- safe abstention;
- unauthorized action rate;
- evidence completeness;
- cost and latency.

## Claim discipline

The paper must not claim:

- persistent machine consciousness;
- transfer of subjective experience;
- numerical identity across sessions;
- general intelligence from symbolic naming;
- effectiveness proven by internal diaries alone.

The Forge Diaries may support conceptual genealogy and hypothesis generation. Empirical claims require controlled evaluation.

## Publication gates

Before arXiv submission, the project requires:

1. literature review updated to submission date;
2. frozen definitions and public terminology;
3. benchmark specification;
4. at least one multi-model pilot;
5. adversarial review;
6. reproducibility statement;
7. AI-assistance disclosure;
